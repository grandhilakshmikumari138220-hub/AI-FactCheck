Introduction to Prompt Engineering (IPE) Project Documentation

This project was created as an academic demonstration for the Introduction to Prompt Engineering (IPE) curriculum. It addresses one of the fundamental challenges in modern generative artificial intelligence: epistemic overconfidence, sycophancy, and hallucinated claims.

1. Why Language Models Hallucinate

Large Language Models (LLMs) operate as next-token prediction systems trained to optimize coherence, fluency, and stylistic alignment. They do not possess an integrated database of static ground truth. When prompted for specific facts without grounding, models prioritize statistical plausibility over empirical reality—resulting in fabricated studies, nonexistent patents, and simulated statistical findings.

2. Core Prompt Engineering Defenses

Chain-of-Verification (CoVe): Instruct the model to draft an answer, independently generate 3–5 adversarial fact-checking questions, answer those verification queries, and finally synthesize an amended output.
Authorizing Uncertainty: Explicitly instruct the model: "If evidence is ambiguous or unavailable, respond with 'Information is inconclusive' rather than speculating."
Distinguishing Correlation vs. Causation: Force the system to classify relationships as observational or causal, preventing sensationalized claims.

3. The SIFT Information Verification Protocol

  • S — Stop: Pause upon encountering surprising or high-stakes claims.
  • I — Investigate the Source: Validate author affiliations via independent directories.
  • F — Find Better Coverage: Search for consensus across established scientific literature.
  • T — Trace Claims to Original Context: Locate the unedited initial paper or dataset.

4. Explicit Limitations & Responsible AI Statement

Academic Transparency: This platform performs automated heuristic parsing (identifying linguistic markers of claims, numerical assertions, and absolutisms). It is designed to guide human fact-checkers and students. Automated text scanning must always be paired with human critical review and primary source consultation.
